מזמינים לערב

Flight Night

Tasting night with friends? Flight Night helps you log every pour, rank them together at the end of the night, and remember what you loved.

  • Plan the night: date, who's coming and who brings what.
  • Snap a label to get style, ABV, bitterness and tasting notes.
  • Everyone ranks privately; the app reveals the winner.
  • Not just beer: whisky, wine, coffee, cheese or anything else.
